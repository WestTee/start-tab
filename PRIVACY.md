# Privacy Policy

Start Tab does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- It reads your browser bookmarks locally to draw the new tab page. Bookmark data never leaves your device.
- It shows site icons using the browser's built-in favicon cache. It makes no network requests of its own.
- It saves page settings (your display name, section colours, collapsed sections, tile density) in the browser's own extension storage, which your browser may sync to your other devices. The developer never receives this data.
- It contains no analytics, tracking or third-party code.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
